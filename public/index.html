Substack has more than 50 million active subscriptions. That’s a lot of profiles to sort through when you need a collab partner. ConnectStack reads bios, matches you by category and collab style, and shows you exactly who you’re looking for, right when you need them.

ConnectStack is free while it’s in beta, and there’s no password to remember. Your email stays private. Your name, publication, bio and Substack link are shown to other members so you can find each other.

        Your data and privacy

        • Other members see your name, publication, bio, categories, collab styles, subscriber count and Substack link, only while “Show me in the directory” is on in My profile.
        • Nobody sees your email. It’s only used to send your sign-in codes.
        • Private to you: your calendar, saved writers, badges and the writers you add from About pages. They’re saved to your account, so they follow you to any device.
        • Shuttle: your chat stays on this device. To answer, your question and your profile and calendar summary are sent to Claude, the AI from Anthropic. Shuttle never messages anyone for you.
        • Voice: Read aloud uses Shuttle’s Heart voice, which runs on your own device after a one-time download, so Shuttle’s answers stay on your device. If you choose “This device” in Display settings, your device’s built-in voice reads instead. If you speak a question, your browser turns it into text (in Chrome, using Google’s speech service).

        Deleting removes your profile, calendar and everything else from ConnectStack for good.
